# Catena: A personal starting point

Catena is deeply personal to me. It brings together many of the things that have shaped my interests and experiences: building things people want to use, thoughtful visual design, gamification, and creating systems that help people.

I have been using Pomodoro apps for years, starting with Forest. They helped me focus when I used them, but opening the app and turning that action into a lasting habit was still difficult. That gap became the starting point for Catena.

During my freshman year, after trying many productivity apps, I designed a “cozy focus” prototype in Figma. I eventually set it aside and built Sugar and Focus, a simpler web app that brought a smaller version of the idea to life. Catena grew from that original exploration.

Through a playful narrative of cats cooking food, Catena makes room for a more caring, sustainable relationship with productivity. I wanted to create an experience people would look forward to returning to, where focusing could feel rewarding without relying on pressure or guilt.
